Academic writing guide

How to write a strong attachment report

Follow your institution's format first. This guide explains what belongs in each chapter, typical lengths, and mistakes to avoid. Write in past tense, first person sparingly, and only state what you personally observed or did.

Formatting standards

  • • A4, 1.5 spacing, 12pt serif body
  • • Numbered chapters & pages
  • • Tables labelled above, figures below
  • • Formal, past-tense language

Evidence to keep

  • • Daily logbook signed by supervisor
  • • Photos / samples (with permission)
  • • Project outputs & appraisals
  • • Attendance & letter of completion

Common mistakes

  • • Vague duties ("I did various tasks")
  • • Copying the company brochure
  • • No challenges or reflection
  • • Missing dates, names & outcomes
